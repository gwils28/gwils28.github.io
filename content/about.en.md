I'm a data scientist with a passion for jazz and a strong taste for statistics. I specialise in time series and causal inference, with solid experience in LLMs and AI agent orchestration. I'm curious about every field where my craft can be applied.

What drives me is building models and systems that help us better understand how things work, and that make better decisions easier to reach. Always up for a good statistical puzzle and attentive to detail, since 2014 I have been developing autonomous systems that predict and simulate complex phenomena with as little bias as possible.

## How I use AI {#ia}

I use AI every day.

**Where it comes in**: assistance with writing code, help understanding some complex
mathematical tools, help with writing and correcting texts, technical and methodological
review, and some visuals, such as cover images.

**What stays mine**: the choice of topics, the protocols, checking the results, the decisions
and the conclusions. The mistakes too.

**My rules**:

- I publish nothing I can't explain or reproduce;
- figures come from code that was run and versioned, never from an AI answer;
- I check the sources I cite myself;
- every piece made with the help of AI carries a transparency note;
- my projects only use public data that anyone can access.
