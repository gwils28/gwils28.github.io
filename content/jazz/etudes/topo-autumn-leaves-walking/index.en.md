beat 3 — at 65 bpm, twice in a row without a mistake.

- [x] **Walking** (25 min): the chart 6 times in a row at 65, click on 2 and 4
- [x] **Play-along** (20 min): one 32-bar chorus without dropping out
- [ ] **Theory** (15 min): II–V–I in B♭, C and F, from memory
