## What I'll share here

Three kinds of notes, sorted by category:

1. **Discovery**: an album, a musician, a concert that stayed with me.
2. **Learning**: what I'm practising on the double bass, the exercises, the
   progress and the roadblocks.
3. **Exploration**: detours into history, theory, or how a tune is built.
