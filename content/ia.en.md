I use AI every day.

**Where it comes in**: assistance with writing code, help understanding some complex
mathematical tools, help with writing and correcting texts, technical and methodological
review, and some visuals, such as cover images.

**What stays mine**: the choice of topics, the protocols, checking the results, the decisions
and the conclusions. The mistakes too.

**My rules**:

- I publish nothing I can't explain or reproduce;
- figures come from code that was run and versioned, never from an AI answer;
- I check the sources I cite myself;
- every piece made with the help of AI carries a transparency note;
- my projects only use public data that anyone can access.
